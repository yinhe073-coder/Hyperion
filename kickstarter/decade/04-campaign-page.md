## Story（正文）

### A calculator that never needs charging

`[GIF：手指转动圆盘，从 1 转到 kg▸lb 标记]`

DECADE is a circular slide rule the size of a large coin. Turn the inner disc, and two logarithmic scales do the arithmetic for you: multiplication, division, unit conversion, percentages, ratios.

It has no battery, no screen and no software. It will work exactly the same in fifty years.

### Why a slide rule, now?

For three centuries, the slide rule was how engineers did math. Bridges, jet engines and the Apollo program were all calculated on one. Then the pocket calculator arrived, and slide rules disappeared almost overnight.

We think something was lost. A slide rule doesn't just give you an answer; it shows you the *relationship*. Set a ratio once, and every pair of numbers around the disc is in that ratio at the same time. You see the whole conversion table at a glance.

DECADE takes the scale of a 5-inch slide rule and wraps it around a 53 mm disc that lives in your pocket, your pencil case or on your desk.

### What it does

`[实拍图：圆盘正面特写，标注各功能区]`

- **Multiply and divide.** Point the inner `1` at a number, find the other number, read the answer.
- **Convert units.** Eight conversion marks are engraved on the outer ring: km ⇄ mi, kg ⇄ lb, in ⇄ cm, m ⇄ ft, oz ⇄ g, gal ⇄ L, nautical miles ⇄ km, hp ⇄ kW. Point at one, and the entire disc becomes a conversion table.
- **Percentages, tips and discounts.** Point at 1.2 and every number is shown with 20% added.
- **Scale anything.** Recipes, maps, exchange rates, model kits. Set the ratio once and read off as many values as you need.
- **°C ⇄ °F.** A fixed double scale on the inner disc, from −40 to 250 °C. No turning needed.
- **π.** Marked on the inner scale for circles and anything round.

### Learn it in 30 seconds

`[GIF 或 4 格图：每一步一张实拍]`

**Multiply 1.5 × 4**
1. Turn the inner disc until its `1` points at `1.5` on the outer ring.
2. Find `4` on the inner disc.
3. Read the outer ring next to it: `6`.

**Convert 70 kg to pounds**
1. Point the inner `1` at the `kg▸lb` mark.
2. Find `7` on the inner disc.
3. Read the outer ring: `1.54`. You know the answer is around 150, so it's 154 lb.

That last step is the one skill a slide rule asks of you: it gives you the digits, and you place the decimal point. It sounds like a limitation. In practice it makes you better at estimating, which is the reason many of us wanted one in the first place.

### How precise is it?

A slide rule of this size reads to about three significant figures. Expect answers within roughly 1% of the exact value. `[打样实测后，把这里换成实测的典型误差]`

That is plenty for checking a bill, converting a recipe, planning a route or sanity-checking a number. It is not a replacement for a calculator when you need eight digits, and we won't pretend it is.

## Risks and challenges（风险与挑战）

> **Feel.** The hardest part of a slide rule is the motion: free enough to turn with one finger, firm enough to hold its setting. We tune this with the washer stack and the pivot tension, and every unit is checked against a torque range before it ships. If production units drift from the prototypes, we will rework them, and that could add a few weeks.
>
> **Legibility.** The smallest numerals are under 1 mm tall. They are sharp on our prototypes, but marking quality depends on the laser and the operator. We inspect every disc and will reject any that are not clearly readable.
>
> **Schedule.** This is a simple product: two cut discs, one pin, no electronics and no moulds. The most likely source of delay is a failed inspection that sends a batch back for rework. We have left `[N]` weeks of slack between the end of production and our delivery estimate.
>
> **Shipping and duties.** Import rules for small parcels have changed several times recently. We have priced US duties into shipping; if the rules change again before we ship, we will tell you what changed and what we are doing about it before any package leaves.
>
> **First campaign.** `[如实写：这是不是你的第一个项目。]` We will post an update at least every two weeks from the end of the campaign until the last reward is delivered, including when the news is bad.

## FAQ

**I've never used a slide rule. Is it hard?**
No. Multiplication takes about thirty seconds to learn, and a quick-start card is included. `[上线后加一个教学视频链接]`

**How accurate is it?**
About three significant figures, typically within 1%. It's a tool for estimating and converting, not for bookkeeping.

**Why are there no square, cube or trig scales?**
Those scales need a sliding cursor to read, which adds thickness and a part that can break. We chose to make the simplest version well. `[如果要做延伸目标，可以把带游标的版本写在这里]`

**Will the markings wear off?**
They are marked into the metal by laser rather than printed on it. `[补充型式试验的真实结果]`

**Can I adjust how stiff it turns?**
The pivot is set at assembly and secured with thread-locker. `[打样后确认是否允许用户自行调节，再写这一条]`

**Which should I choose, steel or titanium?**
They work identically. Steel is heavier and costs less. Titanium weighs about half as much.

**Is it a toy?**
No. It's a precision instrument with small parts, intended for ages 14 and up.

**Where is it made?**
In China, by `[实际地区]` workshops that specialise in laser cutting and marking.

**When will I get it?**
